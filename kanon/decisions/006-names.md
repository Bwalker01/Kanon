# ADR-006: Names

> **Decision:** The engine is Kanon and the marketplace is the Pantheon, following Koine-OS's Greek naming convention.
> **Status:** Proposed. Awaiting owner approval (see `kanon/REVIEW.md`).

## Context

- The working name Mercatus is Latin for "marketplace". The owner requested Greek names: a common word for the engine, at most six letters, easy to spell and pronounce.

## Decision

- **Kanon** (κανών): measuring rod, rule or standard; the root of "canon". It names the engine's two roles: the project's authoritative record and the standard every review measures against.
- **Pantheon:** the marketplace.
- **Agent names:** a mythological name with a plain role, shown together (for example, "Kritos, reviewer"), drawn first from Koine-OS's twelve archetypes. Tools keep their upstream names.
- **Repository:** renamed by the owner to `Bwalker01/Kanon`.

## Basis

- Owner choices (`kanon/sources.md`, section 3).
- Name checks, 2026-11-13: `kanon-engine` is free on PyPI; no notable AI-agent project uses "Kanon".

## Consequences

- Documentation spells the name out where "canon" could be confused.
